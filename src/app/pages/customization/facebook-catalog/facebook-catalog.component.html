help_outline How to set up in Meta Commerce Manager

  1. Go to Meta Commerce Manager and select your business catalog.
  2. In the left sidebar, navigate to Catalog > Data sources and click Add items.
  3. Choose Data feed and click Next.
  4. Select Scheduled feed, paste the CSV Data Feed URL above, and set automatic refresh frequency (e.g. Daily).
  5. Set default currency to BDT and save. Meta will automatically sync all your chosen books into your Facebook & Instagram catalog!
